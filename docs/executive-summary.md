# Executive Summary

This study presents the development of a web-based tracer and employment analytics system for the Alumni Relations and Linkages Office of Central Mindanao University. The proposed CMU ARLO system responds to the need for a centralized, reliable, and accessible platform for managing alumni information, monitoring graduate employment outcomes, and supporting institutional decision-making. Instead of relying on fragmented records and manual processes, the system provides a structured digital environment where alumni data, tracer responses, job-related activities, announcements, feedback, notifications, and reports can be managed in one place.

The general objective of the study is to design and develop a web-based system that improves the efficiency, accuracy, and usefulness of alumni records management. Specifically, the system aims to provide secure account registration and authentication, allow alumni to maintain personal and employment records, support tracer data submission, manage job postings and applications, facilitate feedback and announcements, and generate employment analytics for administrative use. These functions are intended to strengthen alumni engagement while giving the Alumni Relations and Linkages Office better tools for monitoring graduate outcomes and preparing reports.

The study is grounded in key concepts related to alumni tracer systems, employment analytics, web-based information systems, role-based access control, and data privacy. Its conceptual framework follows the Input-Process-Output model, where alumni and administrative inputs are validated, stored, processed, and transformed into useful outputs such as updated records, dashboards, notifications, reports, audit trails, and decision-support information. This framework shows that the system is not only a storage tool but also a platform for improving communication, accountability, and evidence-based planning.

The project follows the Waterfall model as its software development methodology. The development process moves through requirements analysis, system design, implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance. Based on the current system design, the platform uses Laravel as the main application framework, Blade templates for the user interface, PostgreSQL for live application data, Vite and Tailwind CSS for frontend asset handling, and supporting tools for analytics and interface components. The major modules include authentication and account management, alumni profile and settings management, tracer and employment data collection, job workflows, announcements, notifications, feedback handling, analytics, reports, and audit logging.

Overall, the proposed CMU ARLO system is expected to provide significant benefits to the Alumni Relations and Linkages Office, university administrators, alumni, and future researchers. It can reduce manual record-keeping burdens, improve the quality and traceability of alumni information, support faster administrative workflows, and produce employment-related insights that may guide planning, curriculum improvement, and institutional reporting. Although the study is limited to a web-based implementation and does not include external enterprise integrations or automated labor market feeds, it establishes a strong foundation for a scalable, auditable, and data-driven alumni relations platform.
